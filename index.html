THE LAST CITY

A CIVILIZATION
UNDER WATER.

The city has lived beneath an endless artificial rain for so long that nobody remembers a dry sky. The rain is presented as protection. It is not.

People replaced pieces of their bodies with machines, searching for efficiency, durability and survival. Somewhere beneath the streets, a resistance discovered the hidden connection between the rain and the minds of the population.
